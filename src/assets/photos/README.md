## Before you add photos

- Use your own photographs from the real events. Do not use stock images or images you do not have the right to publish.
- Shrink them first. Aim for about 1600 pixels on the long edge and under 400 KB each; free tools such as Squoosh (squoosh.app) do this in seconds. Smaller files make the site load faster.
- Think about the people in the photos. Have permission before publishing images of individuals, and take particular care with images of children.
